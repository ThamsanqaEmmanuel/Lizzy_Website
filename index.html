About LJ Co Traders

LJ CO Traders (Pty) Ltd is a South African-owned company committed to delivering reliable, professional, and cost-effective cleaning and support services to residential, commercial, industrial, and government clients.

We pride ourselves on maintaining high standards of cleanliness, hygiene, safety, and customer satisfaction — providing customised cleaning solutions that meet the unique needs of each client while ensuring a healthy, productive environment.
